# Examination and Re-evaluation Policy

SYNTHETIC DATA: generated for development and testing, not an official AMYPO document.

## Assessment Structure

Each theory course is assessed for 100 marks: 40 internal marks and 60 external marks. The end-semester examination is conducted for 100 marks and scaled to 60.

## Passing Criteria

A student passes a course by scoring at least 30 out of 60 in the end-semester examination and at least 50 out of 100 in total. A course that is not passed is recorded with grade U and counts as a backlog until it is cleared.

## Hall Tickets

Hall tickets are issued only to students with at least 75% attendance in the course (or approved condonation) and no pending fees.

## Re-evaluation

Students may apply for re-evaluation within 7 days of the publication of results. The re-evaluation fee is Rs. 750 per paper. Revised results are published within 21 days. Marks are changed only if the re-evaluated score differs from the original by 5 marks or more.

## Arrear Examinations

Arrear examinations are conducted in June and December. The fee is Rs. 400 per paper. A student may clear a backlog in any later arrear examination.
