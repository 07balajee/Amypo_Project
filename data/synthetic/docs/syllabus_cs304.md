# CS304 Design and Analysis of Algorithms - Syllabus

SYNTHETIC DATA: generated for development and testing, not an official AMYPO document.

## Course Information

- Course code: CS304
- Credits: 4
- Semester: 2025-26 ODD
- Prerequisites: CS201 Data Structures
## Course Objectives

- Analyse the time and space complexity of algorithms.
- Apply divide and conquer, greedy and dynamic programming techniques.
- Understand the limits of computation through NP-completeness.
## Unit 1: Algorithm Analysis

Unit 1 of CS304 covers asymptotic notation, recurrence relations, master theorem, amortized analysis and best, average and worst case.

## Unit 2: Divide and Conquer

Unit 2 of CS304 covers merge sort and quicksort, binary search, Strassen's matrix multiplication, closest pair of points and median of medians.

## Unit 3: Greedy Algorithms

Unit 3 of CS304 covers activity selection, Huffman coding, fractional knapsack, minimum spanning trees: Prim and Kruskal and Dijkstra's shortest path.

## Unit 4: Dynamic Programming

Unit 4 of CS304 covers 0/1 knapsack, longest common subsequence, matrix chain multiplication, Floyd-Warshall algorithm and optimal binary search trees.

## Unit 5: Backtracking and Complexity

Unit 5 of CS304 covers N-queens, graph colouring, branch and bound, P, NP and NP-complete problems and polynomial-time reductions.

## Course Outcomes

- CO1: Analyse algorithms using asymptotic notation.
- CO2: Solve recurrences with the master theorem.
- CO3: Design greedy algorithms and prove their correctness.
- CO4: Design dynamic programming solutions.
- CO5: Prove NP-completeness using reductions.
## Assessment

CS304 is assessed out of 100 marks: 40 internal marks and 60 external marks. Internal marks consist of two Continuous Assessment Tests (CAT 1 and CAT 2) of 15 marks each and assignments worth 10 marks. The end-semester examination is conducted for 100 marks and scaled to 60.

## Textbook

Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest and Stein, Introduction to Algorithms, 4th edition.
