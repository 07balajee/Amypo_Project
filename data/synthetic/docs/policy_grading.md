# Grading and CGPA Policy

SYNTHETIC DATA: generated for development and testing, not an official AMYPO document.

## Letter Grades

- O (Outstanding): 91 to 100 marks, 10 grade points
- A+ (Excellent): 81 to 90 marks, 9 grade points
- A (Very Good): 71 to 80 marks, 8 grade points
- B+ (Good): 61 to 70 marks, 7 grade points
- B (Above Average): 56 to 60 marks, 6 grade points
- C (Average): 50 to 55 marks, 5 grade points
- U (Reappear): below 50 marks, 0 grade points
## SGPA and CGPA

The SGPA is the credit-weighted average of grade points for the courses of one semester: the sum of credits times grade points divided by the sum of credits. The CGPA is calculated the same way over all semesters completed so far. A course with grade U is included only after it is passed.

## Classification

A CGPA of 8.5 or above with no history of backlogs is First Class with Distinction. A CGPA of 6.5 or above is First Class. Any other pass is Second Class.

## Percentage Conversion

The equivalent percentage is calculated as CGPA multiplied by 10.
