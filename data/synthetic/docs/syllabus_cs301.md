# CS301 Database Management Systems - Syllabus

SYNTHETIC DATA: generated for development and testing, not an official AMYPO document.

## Course Information

- Course code: CS301
- Credits: 4
- Semester: 2025-26 ODD
- Prerequisites: CS201 Data Structures
## Course Objectives

- Understand the relational model and the principles of database design.
- Write correct and efficient SQL queries for real-world problems.
- Explain how transactions, concurrency control and recovery keep data consistent.
## Unit 1: Introduction and ER Modelling

Unit 1 of CS301 covers database system architecture, data independence, entity-relationship model, keys and participation constraints and ER to relational mapping.

## Unit 2: Relational Model and Algebra

Unit 2 of CS301 covers relational schema, integrity constraints, relational algebra operators, tuple relational calculus and views.

## Unit 3: SQL

Unit 3 of CS301 covers DDL and DML, joins and subqueries, aggregation with GROUP BY and HAVING, stored procedures and triggers and indexes and query plans.

## Unit 4: Normalization

Unit 4 of CS301 covers functional dependencies, closure and canonical cover, 1NF, 2NF and 3NF, BCNF and lossless decomposition and multivalued dependencies and 4NF.

## Unit 5: Transactions and Recovery

Unit 5 of CS301 covers ACID properties, serializability, two-phase locking, deadlock handling and log-based recovery and checkpoints.

## Course Outcomes

- CO1: Design an ER model for a given requirement.
- CO2: Convert ER models into normalized relations.
- CO3: Write SQL queries including joins, subqueries and aggregates.
- CO4: Analyse schedules for conflict serializability.
- CO5: Explain recovery using logs.
## Assessment

CS301 is assessed out of 100 marks: 40 internal marks and 60 external marks. Internal marks consist of two Continuous Assessment Tests (CAT 1 and CAT 2) of 15 marks each and assignments worth 10 marks. The end-semester examination is conducted for 100 marks and scaled to 60. Lab work carries 20 of the 40 internal marks, and the CAT weight is halved.
